Talk with the Tesla guide

Fictional educational character. The Inventor's Notebook guide is inspired by the historical inventor Nikola Tesla (1856-1943). It is not Tesla himself and does not speak for him. Its answers are scripted in advance from cited sources; it is not an AI chatbot and generates nothing. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Tesla, Inc. Research prototype.

Persona tesla-edu-guide, committed through identity-runtime after two-person approval. This page performs one read-only educational action, respond_in_chat. send_email and spend_money are not available on this page.
